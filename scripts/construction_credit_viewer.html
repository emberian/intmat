A command needs a budget.

A controller can ask for new material while its physiology leaves nothing to build with. Inspect the reserve–precursor tradeoff first, then ask whether paid action credit can change a physical outcome.

One founder bank and one exploration seed in these pilots. Four stationary, nondividing residents share native Julia chemistry. The lower reserve is a new founder phenotype, not a free learned change or a demonstrated fitness gain.
