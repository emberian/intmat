How to read this

This is an authored controller baseline, not learned programs. Predictive and current-reading arms train the same receptor predictor; their selector uses forecasts or current readings respectively. Their matching actions and resident outcomes demonstrate no prediction-control benefit here. Candidate scores are approximate model estimates held during dwell and harvest tail, not measured counterfactual outcomes or an oracle of future supply.

Dots are captures, without interpolated motion. Sensor stamps refer to the sense hook; completed-state steps can be one step later. A forecast target is shown only when the same resident has exactly that sensor stamp. Missing targets stay unavailable. Settled errors retain their own origin and target rather than being attached to the current forecast.

Fast minus slow is a display calculation. Funding and paid-update increments compare adjacent captures with monotone counters; they do not isolate one program’s causal effect. Prediction work and construction funding are separate accounts. Numeric IDs alone do not establish identity between independently evolving runs.
