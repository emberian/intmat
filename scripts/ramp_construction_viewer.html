What this suggests for a living material

A local trend is useful when replenishment remains predictable while a deposit acts. Here catalyst lifetime is 20 time, nominal diffusion across the .18 exchange radius takes .9 time, and the real source continues across the material payoff. A continuously acting policy would need overlapping decision credit, actual local histories, and newborn filter initialization. The living garden’s source density is 14.4 times lower than this assay’s ramp; movement and depletion can break this relationship.

Three engineering levers remain distinct: make the actuator able to fund material; expose information about future substrate; give the predictor enough representational range for attainable returns. A useful learned sign in this ramp should not be transplanted into the earlier static-stock assay, which favored the scarce channel.

Data and method

All outcome dots, funded allocation bars, probe predictions, differences and paid work derive from preserved measured reports. Only the first source-boundary diagram is analytic.
