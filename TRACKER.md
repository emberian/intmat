**Destination:** composable Julia simulations of phenomena inside—and constituting—materials, with evolving inhabitants and light transport revealing their structure.

## What runs

- `bin/interior.jl`: `arena`, `run`, `bigrun`, named `study` commands. Engine in `src/`; recipes in `experiments/`; investigations in `studies/`. `examples/` retains compatibility forwards.
- Ordered `World`/`Experiment` processes with owned state, declared observation ports, preflight checks and timestep bounds. Arbitrary runtime errors do not imply transactional rollback.
- Excitable/recurrent fields, thermal writing, heterogeneous acoustics, dielectric growth, conserved phase separation, irreversible spring fracture and graded-index rays. Coupled ablations connect writing, neural deposition, pressure, ecology and interfaces.
- Inherited recurrent agents with separate lifetime plasticity; finite chemical inventories; heritable metabolic allocation and efficiency/throughput tradeoffs; lower-potential export/uptake; paid shared film that changes transport and phase forcing. Structural recycling does not regenerate fuel.
- Exact sampled state recordings, source-aware executable checkpoints, streamed volume playback, live field/individual inspection, Cycles photography and a curated Pages gallery.

## Evidence and limits

The integrated Julia suite passed 1783 assertions across 101 testsets, including 348 cellular checks and 63 recurrent-material/composition checks. A separate 25-assertion regression covers soak failures, explicit source forks and throughput mode without replacing methods in the shared test process. The shared realFFT phase backend passes 355 focused/coupled assertions and matches the full old 96³ trajectory within 4.88e−17; a 192³ benchmark measured 1.85× speedup. Current Python tools pass 12 tests. Actual bake audits check finite fields, material/work ledgers, graph identity, source hashes and population accounting; photographs receive visual review.

- Small finite-supply metabolic experiments reached capacity 180 then died at sampled t=384–1048; controls varied by seed. [Results](docs/metabolic-experiments.md)
- Strong funded phase feedback retained two substantial positive domains through t=160 versus one in controls; coarsening continued. Persistent compartments remain unproven. [Study](docs/active-compartment-studies.md)
- Periodic fields, optical crops and polished display surfaces are separate. No enclosing deformable gel wall is solved. Older ecology art extrudes 2D; the new collection uses actual 3D populations.
- Inventory units conserve within a discretization. Cross-resolution physical chemistry needs explicit cell-volume normalization.
- Fixed recurrent neural fields are distinct from evolved agents. Attractive morphology, unique genomes and long survival do not establish adaptive organization.
- Pages presents photographs and recorded playback; it does not execute Julia. Large states/scenes and third-party references remain outside Git.

## Holistic alife redesign

The sub-Astra read the coupled implementation and delivered the cellular substrate above. Its analytical bet is that inherited development, persistent physical contacts and exchange between distinct intracellular states open paths to organization that isolated controller particles express poorly. This is a design hypothesis worth developing, not an impossibility theorem about the older models. Short pilots establish working mechanisms and suggest directions; they do not veto longer or differently structured explorations. Historical source-pinned arenas and evidence remain separate.

## Perspective and throughput reset

The original prompts call for neural activity inside, remodeling and constituting
material, with many composable processes and rich 3D interiors. A sparse cell
ecology and polished diagnostic images do not fulfill that ambition. Work now
pairs whole-step cellular optimization (10× target) with GPU-resident multichannel
recurrent material and consequential deposition feedback. Useful hardware
throughput is a design constraint from the outset; all simulation remains Julia.

The [cellular observation exhibit](docs/cellular-observation.md) preserves what the
current runs actually do: early connected assemblies and later renewing, mostly
sparse populations. It includes actual archive states, labeled faithful checkpoint
continuations and two reviewed material photographs. Existing soaks remain pinned.

**Whole-step acceleration:** concrete numerical dispatch, unboxed mechanics,
linked spatial bins and conservative contact-list reuse reduce matched hbox steps
from 29.598→2.585 ms (sparse), 21.228→1.658 ms (bonded), 29.862→3.185 ms
(clustered), and 154.185→12.641 ms (1,024 initial cells). Speedups are
11.45×/12.80×/9.38×/12.20×; every case retains an identical serialized Arena
after 100 steps. Physics, integration bounds and arithmetic order are preserved.
New explicit checkpoint forks can disable runner sleep while retaining resource
controls and their original parent image. [Evidence](docs/cellular-performance.md)

**Recurrent matter:** a fused Julia GPU kernel evolves eight latent channels,
conservative nutrient transport and deposited solid. Solid changes future local
thresholds and communication. Three 128³ compositions reached t60 on Metal and
t150 on AMD; a native 256³ composition reached t60 on Metal. These use fixed
designed rules, not trained/evolved weights. Warm throughput measured 695 million
voxel-steps/s on AMD128³ and 244 million on Metal256³. Increasing this grid at unit
spacing enlarges the domain. Zero-copy resource swaps have a pre-mutation guard
against incompatible fixed aliases. A reviewed native 256³ activity-contour photograph and actual slice motion are published at [the recurrent exhibit](https://emberian.github.io/intmat/recurrent/), bringing the gallery to 60 studies. [Model and retained trajectories](docs/recurrent-material.md)
