Reading this window

Two completed stronger-feedback continuations, model time 3000–3100: all living cells recorded every 0.5 time units. Playback advances those discrete states without interpolating cells. Catalyst and retained-food fields are 32 × 32 samples; the display smooths their spatial texture with periodic edge samples. Each field has its own fixed display range, shared across both recordings. This is a planar material window, with periodic boundaries. The sibling-window button translates the whole periodic domain to place that pair near the center; it does not add or move simulated material.

For compact delivery, fields and controller states use 8-bit display quantization; positions, radii, and battery/body scalars use 16-bit. Internal precursor stocks and realized per-cell flows retain Float32 precision. Colors and neural traces are observations, not an assay of adaptation or cooperation. Per-cell paid flows come from an exact deterministic replay of each physical step. Contributions lost before a post-step observation remain explicitly unassigned in the global account.
