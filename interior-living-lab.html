What this does—and does not—show

The six-channel leaky recurrent rule begins with three hand-structured oscillator pairs plus seeded perturbations. It is not trained, evolved, reproducing, or a model of biological life. Local diffusion communicates neural state; neural activity gates an A+B → P reaction; reaction releases heat; heat changes an irreversible alteration field; alteration affects transport and neural activity. There is no mechanical deformation, bubble interface, wave solver, or light transport here. Latent activity has no energetic cost in this test.

This is an architectural coupling test, not the artistic or numerical limit of the project. The simulator files include complete final 3D fields and neural weights; this viewer stores only quantized slice histories.
