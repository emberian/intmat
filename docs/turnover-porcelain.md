# The hollowing

Ivory and plum contours open into an interior generated by a three-dimensional turnover field. The simulation starts from nearly uniform material with two-percent band-limited noise. There are no drawn strands or seeded organs. Coupled activity spends a finite reserve to build, withdraw, dissolve and relocate material; this is an early transient, followed by coarsening rather than demonstrated stationary organization.

The source is the completed native 128³, extent 24³ run in `outputs/living-tissue-20261015-volume128-5377470/volume`. It records 41 actual states from time 0 to 80 at two-unit intervals, using step size 0.02. The Julia study and its tests are described in `docs/living-tissue.md`; the saved run records the exact source hashes. Its turnover implementation hash is `ca543a8a3eb6e1eb3c93cfc4e16b86f03674ab6ca9598bdf328988ac09f76617`. Native 128³ is the simulation grid, not an upsampled surface. This specimen does not establish spatial convergence, and the later matrix develops a sharper spatial tail.

`scripts/render_turnover_tissue.py` directly polygonizes two saved concentration fields: total structural material `B + M = 0.25`, displayed as ivory porcelain, and persistent matrix `M = 0.16`, displayed as plum glass. A fixed front-right observation wedge exposes the interior. Periodic padding is clipped at the domain boundary. These contours and the cutaway are observation choices, not inferred anatomical membranes or a simulated fracture. Uniform bulk may have no visible internal contour; absence of a contour is not absence of material.

The positive part of the actual fast activity field `x`, multiplied by 0.45, drives a weak chosen warm emission on the ivory contour. The model does not solve luminescence or optical feedback. Materials, thresholds, illumination and phase gain stay fixed across temporal comparisons. Each image records the native dimensions, actual time, camera, source run hash, hashes of every consumed field and the exact renderer hash. The collection cache likewise tracks the selected native fields, so a changed matrix or activity capture cannot silently reuse an earlier photograph.

The wide optical studies compare times 16, 30 and 48 under the same mapping. A close view into the time-30 cutaway is being refined for the still; final publication remains subject to full-resolution visual review. Original field captures and earlier optical studies remain preserved.
