# What the light persuades

This separate epoch closes a loop absent from *The light takes the long way*: recurrent activity changes mineral, mineral bends light, absorbed light drives the recurrent activity. The original artwork and its source snapshots remain intact.

`studies/illuminated_memory.jl` composes the original helical initialization, existing `RecurrentMaterial`, existing GRIN tracer, and two small additions: conservative ray absorption/deposition and a photodrive kernel. No simulation is implemented in Python. Illumination is imposed through three fixed internal apertures; the authored helices are not claimed to emerge spontaneously.

For each traced segment, the midpoint solid density gives Beer–Lambert loss `loss = remaining_power × (1-exp(-alpha × solid × optical_length))`. Alpha is 2 per optical length per solid unit. The optical cube has width 2 while the material cube has width 64, so one optical length equals 32 material lengths. Deposited segment power is distributed with the same cell-centered trilinear weights used to sample solid, then divided by material voxel volume `(64/n)^3`. Thus the field is absorbed power per material volume, not an unnormalized ray hit count. The total incident power is 1000 in declared arbitrary energy/time units, divided equally among rays. Refining the launch grid preserves incident power.

Each material step advances the established recurrent process, then relaxes fast channel 1 toward+1 by the exact frozen-dose factor `1-exp(-gain × dose × dt)`, with gain 4 in material-volume/energy units. This can bias subsequent mineral writing, which remains funded by local nutrient. No mineral source is added. The coupling is a phenomenological light-responsive activity law, not a calibrated photochemical or temperature model. Recomputed ray absorption is held fixed between explicit optical refresh times; the constitutive index law remains the original invented mineral-to-index mapping.

Incident, absorbed, receiver-reaching, and unresolved remaining power are accounted separately. Unresolved means the path exhausted its optical length budget 7; that remainder is neither silently transmitted nor absorbed. Summed trilinear deposition equals calculated absorption before Float32 transfer. Integrated energies use the actual Float32 material timestep. Source snapshots, input hashes, per-refresh power and endpoint ledger, fields, and render settings are retained locally.

The bounded 64³ mechanism gate uses dt 0.025, 240 steps/t=6. Coupled final solid differs 14.22% L2 from a matched dark run and 7.32% from a fixed-initial-dose run. Halving refresh 0.5→0.25 changes final solid 0.643%. Zero-power and zero-absorption controls pass. Increasing illumination 411→1467 rays while reducing ds 0.002→0.0005 at the same 0.25 refresh changes final solid 0.860%. These are local sensitivity tests, not a continuum limit or long-term stability claim. The first production took 4.123 seconds including 12 optical refreshes and captures; the denser pilot took 34.535 seconds.

On the final 64³ coupled field, ds 0.001→0.0005→0.00025 reduces absorbed-dose L2 differences 0.294%→0.144%; mean endpoint differences decrease 0.0103→0.00520 optical units. A sensitive tail retains maximum endpoint changes around 0.23. Individual outgoing paths therefore have a substantially weaker convergence claim than the deposited-energy field; the artwork must preserve this distinction.

Native production uses 256³, 480 steps/t=12, dt0.025, refresh 0.25, 5655 rays per refresh and ds 0.0005. `experiments/art/illuminated-memory.toml` records the exact law and clocks. Native source and fields live under `outputs/collection-20261016/illuminated-memory`, outside Git. The compact publication staging directory removes only the bulky endpoint ledger from its public manifest and pins the complete raw manifest by SHA256; it does not alter the raw epoch.

`render_illuminated_memory.py` reuses the established photograph renderer and adds a contour of the recorded absorbed-power density. Mint color and faint emission identify that measured field, not solved luminescence. The violet mineral skin is an observation material; the underlying GRIN law is distinct from Cycles surface optics. Captured dose is the field held during the preceding material interval, while separately displayed paths are freshly traced through the captured mineral. `build_illuminated_viewer.py` adds actual dose captures to the shared field/ray replay with block means to 64³, log1p followed by fixed 8-bit encoding, and explicit display labeling. Raw dose remains Float32 and unmodified.

Initialization, source copying and the initial capture precede this timer. A second complete native 256³ trajectory at half the ray step, ds=0.00025, took 531.352 seconds. Its final solid differs by only 0.2493% L2 and integrated absorbed energy by 0.01658% from the published epoch. This substantially narrows the material-response uncertainty. It does not establish outgoing-ray endpoint convergence: on the final frozen native field, halving ds=0.0005 changes local absorbed dose by 3.69% L2, mean endpoints by 0.293 and maximum by 6.53 optical units, including paths whose unresolved status changes. The aggregate absorbed-power change is 0.0228%. Report the stable material and aggregate-energy results separately from the sensitive fine ray structure.
