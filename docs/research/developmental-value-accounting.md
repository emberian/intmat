# Local developmental value derived from native allocation

Accessible energy is useful but is not reproductive capacity or fitness. The existing `U=E+η(pA·A+pB·B)` values battery and oxidizable precursor stores. Native growth consumes `g/2` of each precursor and `w_g·g` battery, retains `g` biomass, and records precursor chemical potential as heat. Consequently ideal growth changes U by `−[η(pA+pB)/2+w_g]g`, even when it is productive. The single-resident assay exposed this preference directly: an allocation that reduced uptake and biomass could improve U.

The coefficients come from `cellular_parameters` and the shared `device_anabolic_build!` implementation, including the catalytic habitat's actual affordable growth transaction. Efficiency, precursor potentials and growth work are global physiological parameters in the current native system. Genetic mutation changes neural weights/enzymes and rates, not these coefficients. A future law with heritable efficiency or construction stoichiometry must explicitly account for phenotype revaluation at birth; it cannot call a change of valuation a physical transfer.

Three local constructions distinguish different meanings:

1. **Retained substrate value:** `Vsub=U+λsub·m`, with `λsub=η(pA+pB)/2`. Growth retains the substrate opportunity cost while assembly work remains a cost: `ΔVsub=−w_g g`. It does not assign recoverable chemical energy to biomass.
2. **Replacement-equivalent developmental value:** `Vrep=U+λrep·m`, with `λrep=λsub+w_g`. Ideal growth is neutral: `ΔVrep=0`. This is retained developmental accounting, not an additional physical energy store or an automatic growth reward. Maintenance, secretion, starvation and death still have consequences; the extra maintenance required by larger living bodies is not waived. This is the first proposed new objective because it is linear, local and directly derived from allocation.
3. **Relaxed buildable capacity:** `C=m+min(2A,2B,U/λrep)`. If arbitrary amounts of either precursor may be oxidized, the constraints for new biomass g are `A−oA≥g/2`, `B−oB≥g/2`, and `E+η(pA oA+pB oB)≥w_g g`. Eliminating oxidation variables gives the stated upper bound. Native proportional oxidation, finite rates, energy-capacity overflow, maintenance and competing secretion can prevent attaining it in finite time. It is a local capacity bound, not a theorem about future reproduction or a fitness measure. Its bottleneck plateaus may also produce a less useful learning signal.

For the present chemistry, `η=.65`, `pA=pB=4`, and `w_g=.4`, hence `λsub=2.6` and `λrep=3.0` (implementation uses actual native parameter values). Neither coefficient was selected by optimizing the sign of an experiment. Sensitivity is nevertheless explicit: a measured intervention changes `Vλ` by `ΔU+λΔm`; its sign boundary is `λ*=−ΔU/Δm`. For selected ID5 at lag2, `λ*=1.3304`, while both derived coefficients reverse the apparent U benefit. At λ3 the effect is−2.017e−4. ID6 at lag1 changes sign at1.8970; λ3 gives+1.716e−5. These selected residents do not divide during the compared interval, so those examples need no unrecorded child-mass correction. Other intervals must use the actual partition record.

Native division halves mass, battery and both stores exactly before readout-copy payment. Both linear values, and the homogeneous capacity bound, therefore sum conservatively across parent and child under the current common parameters. A pending parent decision receives the actual child's value at that partition point exactly once for that parent ID. For Vrep this adds `λrep·child_mass` to the existing child-U transfer. Subsequent descendant growth is not credited to that parent, and copied learned marks are neither capital nor a new resource. The actual child copy payment remains a physical cost after the recorded transfer.

Extracellular catalyst is deliberately not assigned replacement credit in this first law. Doing so could reward useless deposition and avoid testing its eventual resource consequences. Likewise cumulative gross growth is not substituted for retained biomass: it could reward futile growth/starvation cycles. An arbitrary colony-size bonus is unnecessary.

The implementation shares the original cohort and affordable learning transaction. Both U and Vrep remain visible in every probe, including dead endpoints and actual birth gifts; physical material/energy ledgers are unchanged. Old U experiments remain valid evidence for their original objective. The causal-flow baseline investigation is orthogonal: on the same original learning trace it helped late windows but worsened the initial transient, so it is not being layered onto the new value law by default.

## Implementation and bounded comparison

`local_value` now accepts an optional concrete developmental coefficient only in the shared cohort maturity/payment context. The absent or zero branch preserves the original arithmetic and does not read biomass, including when a diagnostic mass is NaN. New decision queues store initial U+λm; the native birth hook retains its original U gift and the study wrapper adds actual child mass once, guarded by stable IDs. Endpoint valuation happens inside the original maturity and paid update. There is no temporary queue rewrite at maturity. Calibration, unaffordable payment and invalid identity consume/drop queues through the existing mechanism, so partial advances and complete-step forks cannot double-subtract capital.
