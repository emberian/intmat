# Private construction authority at an actual living checkpoint

The earlier allocation maps perturbed all residents together. A score update for one resident instead depends on that resident's marginal action. `temporal_local_authority.jl` replays the completed slow-growth history learner to time12 using the same recipe, seed6201, paid updates and physical schedule. Recorded per-ID mass, energy, usable inventory and native material/history readings match exactly after decoding Float32 fields at their original precision. An initial audit compared their JSON decimal representations as Float64 and stopped at1.20e−9; its source/output remain preserved. No physical tolerance was relaxed.

Before looking at intervention outcomes, the assay selects IDs5,19,6 by minimum, median and maximum raw balance among the26 live residents. It delegates all sensing, payment, chemistry, mechanics, births and stepping to the existing implementation. Captures occur at lags.2,.5,1,2,4. The compact report retains uptake, catalyst, bound material, conversion, funded/requested construction, own/other returns and survival. It does not infer a general cooperation objective from one checkpoint.

A distinct utility issue is directly visible. ID5's +.3 pulse at lag2 gains1.608e−4 usable inventory while reducing biomass by1.208e−4 and cumulative uptake by1.055e−4. The inverse pulse increases uptake and growth but reduces usable inventory. Thus this objective can reward avoiding productive expenditure; removing baseline variance cannot change that preference. Native growth converts precursor potential to recorded heat and biomass, so biomass is not secretly stored usable energy. Any growth-inclusive or production-oriented reward would be a new declared objective, with its own birth/identity contract, rather than a relabeling of physical U or an accounting subsidy.

The next two questions are now separated: can a causal pre-action flow baseline reduce score noise on the same trace, and does the chosen utility reward the constructive behavior we want? This small assay establishes neither universal weak authority nor an optimal horizon.
