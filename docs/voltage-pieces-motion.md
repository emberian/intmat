# The field gives way

A fresh, bounded temporal relative of **The field inside the fracture**. The fixed camera watches a conducting material develop openings, separate and redirect its electrical interior. Gold potential contours are optical observations of the field inside surviving support, not additional physical sheets. Their color records accumulated Joule dose using one global scale throughout the film.

The native Julia simulation has 34,560 mechanical nodes and 419,228 bonds. It uses the same shared `BondNetwork`, conductivity grains, weak seams, physical scaling, grip forcing, dose weakening and nominal .525 duration as the published still. This fresh run refines the electrical update cadence from 52 to 32 native steps; it is not a replay of the published trajectory or an assertion that its final state is identical. Forty actual observations are captured from t=.108 to .525, each with the current deformed support and newly solved voltage. The film selects 32 contiguous stored observations from t=.194 to .525, avoiding the nearly intact opening. There are no interpolated simulation states, moving cameras, per-frame normalization or invented crack textures.

The new optional snapshot callback in `studies/voltage_between_pieces.jl` is invoked immediately after electrical updates and at the final solve. Immutable bonds/reference geometry are stored once; position, damage, voltage, strain, temperature surrogate and accumulated dose are stored for each observation. These are rendering observations, not complete restart checkpoints. A small unchanged-cadence trajectory is byte-identical with the callback disabled versus read-only observation. The previously added empty-free-set/empty-electrode boundary handling remains in place.

The renderer's optional `--base` and `--dose-max` controls support compact observations and a fixed dose transfer. The exact published still renderer remains privately retained as `outputs/voltage-pieces-48-scaled/render-source-recorded.py`; its source pin and image were not changed. Film optics use the same nine voltage levels (.12 through .90), consistent gradient-facing normals, .004 foil observation thickness, violet boundary section at y=.18, static studio lighting and camera at (2.8,-4,2.6), orthographic scale 2.85. The entire sequence uses global dose maximum .03901355913548854 with the existing 16-shade palette. All changing shape and color comes from the actual observations.

The 1,554-step CPU2 simulation, including capture, took 108.80 seconds. Across all forty observations, the maximum independent normalized edge-current residual is 3.46e-7; relative source/Joule power defect is at most 4.04e-6; mechanical work-ledger residual is at most .06718%. Damage is irreversible and dose is nondecreasing. Displayed observations span 58 to 28,736 broken bonds. Prescribed electrodes and grips supply power; the temperature/dose law remains a nondimensional surrogate without latent-heat debit or closed coupled thermodynamic accounting. Reference-length conductance and the conservative six-live-edge tetrahedral observation criterion retain the qualifications described in `docs/voltage-pieces.md`.
