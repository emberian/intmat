# Parameter scouting: arrested traces and colliding fronts

2026-10-14, Codex scout. All specimens use the existing five Gaussian excitations from `build_scene`, the existing excitable/transport/deposition processes, and seed 17. No geometry was added. Particle carriers were disabled to isolate field dynamics. Six 32² trials ran to t20–30, followed by three 40³ trials; the latter matter because spherical excitations dilute faster than planar ones.

`outputs/scout/comparison.png` compares raw deposit fields at a common color scale. High feedback (2.5–3) extinguishes the original spots. Low threshold/feedback permits a traveling excursion that ultimately fills the domain. The 2D brake trial (threshold .12, recovery .12, feedback 1.5, deposition .5) preserves a five-lobed ring with a central void at t10–20. Its direct 3D transfer quenches, as does a second attempt with diffusion .0015 and threshold .10.

The selected 3D trial is `experiments/materials/lace.toml`, saved at `outputs/scout/lace-v3`: diffusion .0015, threshold .08, recovery .07, material feedback .8, deposition .5; weak wave activity gain .04. At t4 the five excitable regions remain separate. At t7 their expanded fronts touch around an open central gap; at t10 the gap starts closing. This is a useful **transient collision specimen**, not evidence of stable cellular lace or self-organized persistent shells. Prefer frame-0350 (t7) for rendering. `projections.png` shows the progression.
