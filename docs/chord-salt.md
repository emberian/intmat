# A chord held in salt

New preserved variant of **Oblique fronts**. Three prepared coherent pressure packets interfere inside a finite mineral precursor. The resulting pressure-squared envelope converts precursor into slower material; the converted structure changes subsequent wave propagation. This is a nondimensional acoustic/material artwork, not calibrated salt crystallization.

The study uses the production `AcousticField` directly: periodic scalar waves, symmetric arithmetic mineral-dependent face speed, damped centered time integration, exponential intensity envelope. Initial zero velocity uses the half-acceleration initialization. No ongoing acoustic source is applied. Local conversion is an exact exponential fraction of remaining precursor, with nutrient reconstructed as its complement to limit Float32 inventory drift. Conversion does not debit acoustic or latent energy; changing wave speed and damping make this an open phenomenological law. No closed thermodynamic claim is made.

Authored inputs are three angled Gaussian-enveloped packets, relative phases, and an ellipsoidal precursor with a diagonal opening. Internal fringes and cavities of the converted mineral are evolved state. The selected mesh is the actual mineral level set at concentration .55, exactly plane-clipped at y=-.04 without caps. The precursor support is independently plane-clipped at y=.12 to form an open amber back optical shell. This is an explicit photographic cutaway; no field is smoothed or reconstructed. Matte navy mineral optics and broad neutral lights reveal the interference relief against a floorless charcoal background. Optical coefficients are artistic assignments, not calibrated scattering measurements. An earlier glossy variant with polygon-rejection cuts is retained privately and excluded from publication.

Sources: `studies/chord_salt.jl`, `studies/chord_salt_checks.jl`, `scripts/render_chord_salt_section.py` (selected) and `scripts/render_chord_salt.py` (private rejected variant). Native data and Blender outputs remain private in `outputs/chord-salt-*`. No historical run is migrated or changed.
